Workflow

Settings

Settings are grouped into General, Tags, and Feedback tabs.

General

General controls Save payload, Save scope, and whether Command-S also saves referenced SwiftTag documents.

Tags

Tags controls number padding, total-count keys, automatic track total, compilation propagation, and picture propagation.

Feedback

Feedback controls save notifications, app theme, and difference/status colors.
